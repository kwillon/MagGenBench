## MagGenBench preparation and packaging

The released files are a selected and arranged subset, not the original complete source archive. Preparation includes the retained-record selection, grouped train/validation/test assignments, CSV layout and source-local identifiers, the saved spin-polarization/volume target, five CrystalFormer representation exclusions, and CrystaLLM CIF-text normalization/tokenization. Historical preparation-code and training-use limitations are stated in the README.

The audited prepared file bytes are preserved in this release, including existing numeric strings, CIF text and split membership. Earlier normalization and CrystalFormer decimal reserialization are explicitly documented. No raw database archive, program source, pretrained weights or NEMAD rows are part of this curated data release.
