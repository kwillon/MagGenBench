## Figure 8. Multi-objective structural and composition-based
comparison of ten crystal-generator cohorts

(A) Structural QC retention, unique reduced-formula yield,
and 3d-containing yield, expressed relative to a nominal
generation budget of 2,000 structures per cohort.

(B) Structural retention versus unique-formula yield.
Marker colors indicate the model cohorts, marker shapes
distinguish Base, Fine-tuned, and Refined variants, and
marker sizes reflect 3d-containing yield. Dark outlines
identify Pareto-efficient cohorts.

(C) Distributions of generator ranks across 20,000 weight
combinations sampled from Dirichlet(1,1,1), applied to
min-max-normalized objectives.

(D) Frequency with which each cohort ranked among the five
highest-scoring models across sampled weight combinations.

Ranking frequencies describe sensitivity to the selected
objectives and weighting scheme, not probabilities of
intrinsic model superiority. The 3d-containing yield is
a compositional proxy rather than validated magnetic
functionality. Generation budgets remain nominal pending
verification against original generation records.
