# Figure 5 caption (for insertion into the external manuscript)

**Figure 5. Predicted energy-magnetism landscape across ten structurally
retained benchmark cohorts.** (A) Pooled greyscale hexagonal density of all
12,277 finite paired CHGNet total-energy and DXMag moment predictions from
12,281 QC-retained structures; four rows without finite pairs are excluded.
The colorbar reports structures per hexagon on a logarithmic scale. This
pooled panel does not distinguish generators; generator-specific comparisons
are shown in B-F. (B-F) MatterGen, Con-CDVAE, CrystalFormer, CrystaLLM and
Crys-JEPA, respectively, each use one family hue. Circles show Base predictions
and triangles show fine-tuned predictions (Refined for Crys-JEPA). Every finite
pair is shown without subsampling or quantile clipping, with common axis limits.
Filled diamonds mark Base population medians, and open diamonds mark adapted
population medians. Arrows point from Base to adapted group medians, not along
matched-sample trajectories. Energies are CHGNet-predicted total energies in
eV/atom, not formation energies or convex-hull stability. DXMag moments in
mu_B/f.u. are composition-based predictions and do not identify magnetic ground
states.
