## Figure 8. Multi-objective structural and composition-based
comparison of ten crystal-generator cohorts

(A) QC retention, unique reduced-formula yield, and
3d-transition-metal-containing yield, expressed relative to an
assumed nominal budget of 2,000 generated structures per cohort.

(B) Structural retention versus unique-formula yield. Marker
color identifies the model family and variant; marker shape
distinguishes Base, Fine-tuned, and Refined. Dark outlines identify
cohorts on the three-objective Pareto frontier. Marker size reflects
3d-containing yield.

(C) Rank-frequency distributions for 20,000 weight vectors
sampled from Dirichlet(1,1,1), using min-max-normalized metrics.

(D) Frequency with which each cohort ranked first among the
sampled weight vectors.

The 3d-containing yield is a composition-based proxy, not an
independently validated magnetic-property measurement.
Weight-sensitivity frequencies are conditional on the chosen
objective set, normalization, and weight distribution.

The equal generation-budget assumption is not yet independently
verified for every cohort. Accordingly, quantitative yields and
rankings remain provisional pending provenance verification.
